# Contributing to Gray Oak Academy

Thank you for considering a contribution. Gray Oak Academy is source-available
under the [Elastic License 2.0](LICENSE.md). Bug reports and focused,
high-quality pull requests are welcome.

## Licensing of contributions

Unless the Project Owner expressly identifies a different license for a
particular portion of the project before submission, the Applicable Project
License for new Contributions is the [Elastic License 2.0](LICENSE.md). A
license applying solely to a third-party font, dependency, media file, example,
copied component, or other third-party material does not become the Applicable
Project License for a Contribution merely because that material is present in
the repository.

## Before opening a pull request

- Keep each pull request focused and explain the problem it solves.
- Add or update tests when behavior changes.
- Run the relevant tests and `rubocop`, and report the commands and results in
  the pull request.
- Do not include secrets, private information, or generated credentials.
- Identify all third-party code, media, or other material, including its owner,
  source, and license. Do not include substantive third-party material without
  the Project Owner's approval. Acceptance of a pull request does not constitute
  approval of third-party material that was not properly disclosed.
- Review and understand any material produced by generative AI, and confirm that
  no applicable terms, confidentiality obligations, or third-party rights
  prevent you from submitting it under the CLA.

For security vulnerabilities, do not open a public issue or pull request. Email
<lucas@grayoak.net> privately.

## Contributor License Agreement

Before an individually owned contribution can be accepted, merged, or
incorporated, every individual who authored a copyrightable portion must read
and accept the [Individual Contributor License
Agreement](CONTRIBUTOR_LICENSE_AGREEMENT.md).

The person opening the pull request accepts the CLA directly in the pull-request
description by replacing `[FULL LEGAL NAME]` with their full legal name and
checking the acceptance box, unless they have already accepted Version 1.0 and
that acceptance remains in effect. Complete the section before submitting the
pull request. A maintainer must not complete or edit a contributor's signature
block.

Each additional co-author who has not already accepted Version 1.0 must accept
the CLA by posting the following statement from their own GitHub account as a
comment on the pull request, replacing `[FULL LEGAL NAME]` with their full legal
name:

> I, [FULL LEGAL NAME], have read and agree to the Gray Oak Academy Individual
> Contributor License Agreement, Version 1.0. I intend this comment, including my
> typed legal name, as my electronic signature.

The completed pull-request section and any co-author comments create public
acceptance records without requiring a separate account or CLA service. The
Project Owner may retain copies of those records.

Automated dependency-update pull requests are exempt from the CLA only to the
extent that they change dependency manifests, lockfiles, checksums, or version
references without adding substantive human-authored material. Updates that add
or replace vendored source code, generated code, media, or other substantive
third-party material remain subject to the third-party-material review
requirements. Any human-authored changes added to an automated pull request
remain subject to the CLA.

Each contributor's legal name and acceptance record will be public. A
pseudonymous GitHub account may contribute only if the contributor includes
their full legal name; anonymous CLA acceptance is not permitted.

The Individual CLA does not cover entity-owned contributions. If an employer or
another entity may own or restrict the rights in a contribution, do not submit
it. The project cannot accept, merge, or incorporate it without an appropriate
entity contributor agreement or other written authorization satisfactory to the
Project Owner.

A contributor under eighteen years old must not use the GitHub acceptance
block. Their parent or legal guardian must contact <lucas@grayoak.net> before
the Contribution is submitted so that a separate written agreement can be
arranged.

## Patent-relevant contributions

The CLA grants patent licenses; it does not assign ownership of an invention,
patent application, or patent. If you believe a contribution or technical
proposal may contain novel or patentable subject matter, do not disclose its
technical details in a public issue or pull request. First email
<lucas@grayoak.net> privately and identify the potential patent relevance.

A contributor's disclosure does not determine patentability; the Project Owner
may flag any Contribution for patent review. No Contribution flagged for patent
review may be merged, incorporated, or used in an implementation until the
Project Owner determines in writing whether a separate invention or patent
assignment is necessary. If an assignment is required, the Project Owner will
provide a separate written agreement, which must be fully executed before the
Contribution is merged, incorporated, or used.

## Pull request review

Acceptance of the CLA does not guarantee that a contribution will be reviewed,
accepted, or maintained. Reviews may consider product direction, teacher
control, security, maintainability, test coverage, and consistency with the
project's architecture and license.
